# 15-minute diagnostic

Eight original items, one from each of the eight topics, to show you where to start. Set a timer for 15 minutes, answer everything without looking anything up, then check your answers and read the table at the bottom.

These items are original, written for these notes. They are not taken from the Bennett Mechanical Comprehension Test or any publisher's materials. The real test shows a picture with every question. These items are in words so you can do them anywhere, with a pencil and paper for the arithmetic.

If you already know the basics of each topic, skip this and take the [free Bennett Mechanical practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/bennett-mechanical?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=bennett-mechanical) instead. A full timed run gives a better picture of pacing.

## Part A: Levers and balance

**D1.** A 50 kg child sits 3 m from the pivot of a seesaw. How far from the pivot, on the other side, must a 75 kg adult sit to balance it?

- A. 2 m
- B. 3 m
- C. 4.5 m

## Part B: Gears, belts and wheels

**D2.** A 12-tooth gear turning at 90 rpm drives a 36-tooth gear. How fast does the 36-tooth gear turn?

- A. 270 rpm
- B. 30 rpm
- C. 90 rpm

## Part C: Pulleys, ramps, screws and wedges

**D3.** A single movable pulley, with one end of its rope tied to a beam, lifts a 300 N load. Ignoring friction and the pulley's weight, what pull is needed?

- A. 150 N
- B. 300 N
- C. 600 N

## Part D: Fluids and hydraulics

**D4.** A hydraulic jack has a small piston of 2 cm² and a large piston of 40 cm². A 100 N push on the small piston can lift what load on the large piston?

- A. 2000 N
- B. 200 N
- C. 80 N

## Part E: Heat and temperature

**D5.** A house is heated only by radiators on the ground floor, yet the upstairs rooms are often warmer. Why?

- A. Warm air rises
- B. Cold air rises
- C. Heat travels downward through floors

## Part F: Electricity

**D6.** Three identical bulbs are connected in parallel to a battery. One bulb is unscrewed. What happens to the other two?

- A. They go out
- B. They get dimmer
- C. They stay just as bright

## Part G: Motion and forces

**D7.** A bus moving forward brakes suddenly. Which way do standing passengers lurch?

- A. Forward
- B. Backward
- C. Sideways

## Part H: Sound, light, shapes and volumes

**D8.** One cube-shaped tank measures 1 m along each edge. Another measures 2 m along each edge. How many times more water does the larger tank hold?

- A. 2 times
- B. 4 times
- C. 8 times

<details>
<summary>Answers and explanations, D1 to D8</summary>

**D1: A (2 m).** 50 x 3 = 150, and 75 x 2 = 150. The heavier person sits closer to the pivot.

**D2: B (30 rpm).** Teeth x rpm stays the same: 12 x 90 = 36 x 30. The bigger gear turns more slowly.

**D3: A (150 N).** Two rope segments hold up the movable pulley, so each carries half the load.

**D4: A (2000 N).** The large piston has 40 / 2 = 20 times the area, so it gives 20 times the force: 100 x 20 = 2000 N.

**D5: A.** Air warmed by the radiators rises up the stairs and collects in the upper rooms. This is convection.

**D6: C.** In parallel, each bulb has its own path to the battery. Removing one leaves the others' paths unchanged.

**D7: A (forward).** The bus slows, but the passengers' bodies keep moving forward until something stops them.

**D8: C (8 times).** 1 x 1 x 1 = 1 cubic metre against 2 x 2 x 2 = 8 cubic metres. Doubling every edge multiplies the volume by eight.

</details>
