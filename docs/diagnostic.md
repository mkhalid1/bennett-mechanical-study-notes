# 15-minute diagnostic

Twenty-four original items, three from each of the eight topics, to show you where to start. Set a timer for 15 minutes, answer everything without looking anything up, then check your answers and read the table at the bottom.

These items are original, written for these notes. They are not taken from the Bennett Mechanical Comprehension Test or any publisher's materials. The real test shows a picture with every question. These items are in words so you can do them anywhere, with a pencil and paper for the arithmetic.

If you already know the basics of each topic, skip this and take the [free Bennett Mechanical practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/bennett-mechanical?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=bennett-mechanical) instead. A full timed run gives a better picture of pacing.

## Part A: Levers and balance

**D1.** A 50 kg child sits 3 m from the pivot of a seesaw. How far from the pivot, on the other side, must a 75 kg adult sit to balance it?

- A. 2 m
- B. 3 m
- C. 4.5 m

**D2.** Two bolt cutters have identical jaws. One has short handles and one has long handles. Which cuts a thick bolt with less effort?

- A. The short-handled one
- B. The long-handled one
- C. Both need the same effort

**D3.** A plank rests on two supports, P and Q, 6 m apart. A 900 N load sits 2 m from P and 4 m from Q. Ignoring the plank's weight, how much of the load does Q carry?

- A. 300 N
- B. 450 N
- C. 600 N

## Part B: Gears, belts and wheels

**D4.** Five gears are in a row, each meshing with the next. The first turns clockwise. Which way does the fifth turn?

- A. Clockwise
- B. Anticlockwise
- C. You cannot tell without knowing the sizes

**D5.** A 12-tooth gear turning at 90 rpm drives a 36-tooth gear. How fast does the 36-tooth gear turn?

- A. 270 rpm
- B. 30 rpm
- C. 90 rpm

**D6.** Two pulleys are joined by an open belt that is not crossed. The driving pulley turns clockwise. Which way does the other pulley turn?

- A. Anticlockwise
- B. It does not turn
- C. Clockwise

## Part C: Pulleys, ramps, screws and wedges

**D7.** A single movable pulley, with one end of its rope tied to a beam, lifts a 300 N load. Ignoring friction and the pulley's weight, what pull is needed?

- A. 150 N
- B. 300 N
- C. 600 N

**D8.** A frictionless ramp is 6 m long and 2 m high. What force along the ramp pushes a 600 N crate up it at a steady speed?

- A. 600 N
- B. 300 N
- C. 200 N

**D9.** A block and tackle has three rope segments holding up the moving block. To raise the load by 1 m, how much rope must you pull through?

- A. 1 m
- B. 3 m
- C. A third of a metre

## Part D: Fluids and hydraulics

**D10.** Why are dams built much thicker at the bottom than at the top?

- A. The bottom only has to carry the weight of the dam itself
- B. Water pressure is greatest at the bottom
- C. Thick bottoms stop the water freezing

**D11.** A hydraulic jack has a small piston of 2 cm² and a large piston of 40 cm². A 100 N push on the small piston can lift what load on the large piston?

- A. 2000 N
- B. 200 N
- C. 80 N

**D12.** Water runs gently from a garden hose. You partly cover the end with your thumb. The water now comes out:

- A. More slowly
- B. Faster
- C. At the same speed

## Part E: Heat and temperature

**D13.** Why do many saucepans have wooden or plastic handles?

- A. Wood and plastic are lighter than metal
- B. Wood and plastic conduct heat poorly
- C. Wood and plastic expand more than metal

**D14.** Overhead power cables are hung between the same two poles all year. When do they hang lowest, with the most sag?

- A. On a hot summer day
- B. On a cold winter day
- C. They sag the same all year

**D15.** A house is heated only by radiators on the ground floor, yet the upstairs rooms are often warmer. Why?

- A. Warm air rises
- B. Cold air rises
- C. Heat travels downward through floors

## Part F: Electricity

**D16.** Three identical bulbs are connected in parallel to a battery. One bulb is unscrewed. What happens to the other two?

- A. They go out
- B. They get dimmer
- C. They stay just as bright

**D17.** A 9 V battery is connected across a 3 ohm resistor. What current flows?

- A. 27 A
- B. 3 A
- C. 6 A

**D18.** A torch takes two identical batteries in series. One is put in the wrong way round. What happens?

- A. The bulb is brighter than normal
- B. The bulb does not light
- C. The bulb is as bright as normal

## Part G: Motion and forces

**D19.** A coin and a feather are dropped together inside a tall tube with all the air pumped out. Which reaches the bottom first?

- A. The coin
- B. They arrive together
- C. The feather

**D20.** A bus moving forward brakes suddenly. Which way do standing passengers lurch?

- A. Forward
- B. Backward
- C. Sideways

**D21.** A spring stretches 4 cm under an 8 N load. What load stretches it 10 cm, if it is not overloaded?

- A. 20 N
- B. 16 N
- C. 40 N

## Part H: Sound, light, shapes and volumes

**D22.** One cube-shaped tank measures 1 m along each edge. Another measures 2 m along each edge. How many times more water does the larger tank hold?

- A. 2 times
- B. 4 times
- C. 8 times

**D23.** You stand 1 km from a fireworks display. Sound travels at about 340 m per second. About how long after you see a burst do you hear the bang?

- A. At the same moment
- B. About 3 seconds
- C. About 30 seconds

**D24.** A ray of light hits a flat mirror straight on, at 90 degrees to its surface. Where does it go?

- A. Straight back the way it came
- B. Along the surface of the mirror
- C. Off at 45 degrees

<details>
<summary>Answers and explanations, D1 to D24</summary>

**D1: A (2 m).** 50 x 3 = 150, and 75 x 2 = 150. The heavier person sits closer to the pivot.

**D2: B.** Longer handles give your hands a longer arm about the pivot, so the same squeeze produces more force at the jaws.

**D3: A (300 N).** The load is closer to P, so P carries more. Q's share = 900 x 2 / 6 = 300 N, and P carries the other 600 N.

**D4: A (clockwise).** Each mesh reverses direction. From gear 1 to gear 5 there are four meshes, an even number, so gear 5 turns the same way as gear 1. Sizes do not affect direction.

**D5: B (30 rpm).** Teeth x rpm stays the same: 12 x 90 = 36 x 30. The bigger gear turns more slowly.

**D6: C (clockwise).** An open belt keeps the same direction. Only a crossed belt reverses it.

**D7: A (150 N).** Two rope segments hold up the movable pulley, so each carries half the load.

**D8: C (200 N).** Force = 600 x 2 / 6 = 200 N.

**D9: B (3 m).** Each of the three supporting segments must shorten by 1 m, so 3 m of rope passes through your hands. A third of the force, three times the distance.

**D10: B.** Pressure grows with depth, so the water pushes hardest on the bottom of the dam.

**D11: A (2000 N).** The large piston has 40 / 2 = 20 times the area, so it gives 20 times the force: 100 x 20 = 2000 N.

**D12: B (faster).** The same water must get through a smaller opening, so it speeds up.

**D13: B.** Metal conducts heat quickly to your hand. Wood and plastic are poor conductors, so the handle stays cool enough to hold.

**D14: A.** Metal cables expand in the heat, so they get slightly longer and sag lower in summer.

**D15: A.** Air warmed by the radiators rises up the stairs and collects in the upper rooms. This is convection.

**D16: C.** In parallel, each bulb has its own path to the battery. Removing one leaves the others' paths unchanged.

**D17: B (3 A).** Current = voltage / resistance = 9 / 3 = 3 A.

**D18: B.** The reversed battery pushes against the other one. With identical batteries their pushes cancel, so no current flows.

**D19: B.** With no air, there is no air resistance, and gravity speeds both up at the same rate.

**D20: A (forward).** The bus slows, but the passengers' bodies keep moving forward until something stops them.

**D21: A (20 N).** The spring stretches 1 cm for every 2 N, so 10 cm needs 20 N.

**D22: C (8 times).** 1 x 1 x 1 = 1 cubic metre against 2 x 2 x 2 = 8 cubic metres. Doubling every edge multiplies the volume by eight.

**D23: B (about 3 seconds).** 1000 / 340 is just under 3. Light arrives almost instantly.

**D24: A.** The angle out equals the angle in. A ray at 90 degrees to the mirror comes straight back.

</details>
