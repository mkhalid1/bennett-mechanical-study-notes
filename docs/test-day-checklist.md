# Test-day checklist

A short list for the day you sit the BMCT-II online. Your invitation from the employer always overrides anything here, so read it first.

## The day before

- Read your invitation again: the deadline, the link, and any rules about where and how to sit the test.
- Plan to use a **desktop or laptop with a dependable internet connection**. Pearson recommends this over a phone ([Pearson TalentLens](https://www.talentlens.com/bennett-mechanical.html)).
- If you need adjustments, for example because of a disability, ask the employer before you start, not after.
- Do one short warm-up set of mixed items, then stop. No late-night cramming.

## One hour before

- Find a quiet room where nobody will interrupt you for 30 minutes.
- Close other programs and browser tabs. Silence your phone and put it out of reach.
- Have scrap paper and a pencil ready for ratios and quick sketches, unless your invitation says otherwise. Pearson's page does not publish a rule on calculators or paper, so follow the employer's instructions.
- Plug in your laptop.

## In the first minute

- Read the instructions on screen carefully, even if you have practised. Check how to move between items and whether you can go back.
- Note the start time so you can use the [clock checkpoints](timing.md#clock-checkpoints).

## During the test

- **Read the question before studying the picture.** Then look only at the part of the picture the question is about.
- **Name the rule.** Lever, gear direction, rope count, depth, series or parallel. Most items need only one.
- **Check arrows and labels.** One arrow or one number often decides the answer.
- **Watch the clock at 5, 10, 15 and 20 minutes.** About 11 items every 5 minutes keeps you on track.
- **Do not get stuck.** Past 45 seconds, choose your best answer and move on.
- **Answer every item.** There is no published penalty for wrong answers.

## Afterwards

- Write down which topics felt hardest while you still remember. If you are asked to resit, or meet another mechanical test later, that list is where to start.
- Do not try to reconstruct or share the questions. They come from a secure item bank, and sharing them may break the employer's rules.

## Last practice before the real one

If you have time for one more run, take the [free Bennett Mechanical practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/bennett-mechanical?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=bennett-mechanical) under real timing, at the same time of day as your test.
