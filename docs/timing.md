# Timing and pacing

The BMCT-II gives you 55 items in 25 minutes ([Pearson TalentLens](https://www.talentlens.com/bennett-mechanical.html)). That is the tightest part of the test for most people. Knowing the physics is not enough: you need to recognise each type of item fast enough to leave time for the harder ones.

Everything on this page apart from the 55 items and 25 minutes is our study guidance, not a publisher rule.

## The budget

| | |
|---|---|
| Total time | 25 minutes, which is 1,500 seconds |
| Items | 55 |
| Average per item | About 27 seconds |
| Realistic split | About 15 to 20 seconds on familiar items, 40 to 45 seconds on the few that need thought or arithmetic |

You will not spend the same time on every item, and you should not try to. A gear-direction item takes 10 seconds once the rule is automatic. A hydraulic-press item with areas may need 30. The time you save on quick items pays for the slow ones.

## Clock checkpoints

Glance at the clock at these points. If you are behind, speed up on the next few items rather than panicking at the end.

| Time used | Aim to have answered |
|---|---|
| 5 minutes | About 11 items |
| 10 minutes | About 22 items |
| 15 minutes | About 33 items |
| 20 minutes | About 44 items |
| 25 minutes | All 55, with every item answered |

## Topic by topic

| Topic | Typical time | Why |
|---|---|---|
| [Gears, belts and wheels](sections/gears-belts-and-wheels.md) | 10 to 25 s | Direction is counting. Speed is one ratio. |
| [Motion and forces](sections/motion-and-forces.md) | 10 to 20 s | One rule per item, but watch for trap answers |
| [Heat and temperature](sections/heat-and-temperature.md) | 10 to 15 s | Name the process and the answer follows |
| [Levers and balance](sections/levers-and-balance.md) | 15 to 25 s | One multiplication each side |
| [Pulleys, ramps, screws and wedges](sections/pulleys-and-inclined-planes.md) | 15 to 25 s | Count ropes, then divide |
| [Sound, light, shapes and volumes](sections/sound-light-and-shapes.md) | 15 to 25 s | Volumes need arithmetic |
| [Fluids and hydraulics](sections/fluids-and-hydraulics.md) | 15 to 25 s | Most need no numbers. Presses need one ratio. |
| [Electricity](sections/electricity.md) | 15 to 25 s | Trace the loop first |

## Where time usually goes

- **Re-reading the picture.** Read the question first, then look at the picture for the part it asks about. You do not need to understand the whole drawing.
- **Doing arithmetic you do not need.** Many items can be answered from the direction of an effect alone. If only one option goes the right way, pick it.
- **Second-guessing trap items.** If you have applied the rule, trust it and move on.
- **One stubborn item.** If you are past 45 seconds, choose your best answer, note the item number if the platform lets you return, and move on.

## Practise against the clock

- **Drill topics in short bursts.** Do 10 items from one topic in 3 minutes. That builds the recognition speed.
- **Then mix topics.** Switching between gears, circuits and fluids is harder than doing ten of one kind. The real test mixes them.
- **Take at least two full timed runs** before the test. The [free Bennett Mechanical practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/bennett-mechanical?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=bennett-mechanical) has 55 items and a 25-minute timer, the same length as the real test.
- **Review by topic.** After each timed run, look at which topic cost the most time, not only which items you got wrong.
