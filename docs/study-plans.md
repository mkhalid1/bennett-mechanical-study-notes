# Study plans

Two plans: one for a week, one for when the invitation has already arrived. Both start with the [15-minute diagnostic](diagnostic.md) so you know which topics to fix first. Expect 45 to 90 minutes a day on the 7-day plan.

## 7-day plan

| Day | Focus | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline | Read the [test overview](test-overview.md). Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). Note your three weakest topics. |
| 2 | Levers, pulleys and ramps | Read the [levers](sections/levers-and-balance.md) and [pulleys](sections/pulleys-and-inclined-planes.md) guides and do both item sets. These two share one idea: force traded for distance. |
| 3 | Gears and motion | Read the [gears](sections/gears-belts-and-wheels.md) and [motion](sections/motion-and-forces.md) guides. Do both item sets. Say the direction rule out loud until it is automatic. |
| 4 | Fluids, heat and electricity | Read the [fluids](sections/fluids-and-hydraulics.md), [heat](sections/heat-and-temperature.md) and [electricity](sections/electricity.md) guides. Write the rules from each on one card. |
| 5 | First timed run | Read the [sound, light and shapes](sections/sound-light-and-shapes.md) guide. Then take a full timed practice test, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/bennett-mechanical?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=c6wnky&utm_content=bennett-mechanical). Read the [timing guide](timing.md). |
| 6 | Fix the weakest topics | Go back to the two topics that cost you most on day 5. Redo their items without looking at the answers. Reread their traps lists. |
| 7 | Second timed run and rest | Take a second full timed test in the morning. Compare with day 5. Read the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). Stop studying by early evening. |

### How to review a missed item

The review is where the score comes from. For each miss, answer three questions:

1. **Which rule did I break?** For example: measured from the wrong point, counted the wrong ropes, thought heavy things fall faster.
2. **What in the picture should have stopped me?** Name the label, arrow or number.
3. **What will I check next time?** One short habit, such as "count meshes before picking a direction".

Keep these in one list. By day 6 you will see the same two or three mistakes repeating. Fixing those is worth more than any number of new items.

## 2-day plan

For when the test link has arrived and the deadline is close.

### Day 1: learn the rules

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15 min | Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). |
| 2 | 30 min | Read the "What it measures" and "Traps" parts of all eight topic guides. Skip the rest for now. |
| 3 | 40 min | Do the practice items for your three weakest topics. Read every explanation, including for items you got right. |
| 4 | 25 min | Take a full timed practice test, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/bennett-mechanical?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=c6wnky&utm_content=bennett-mechanical). |
| 5 | 20 min | Review every miss with the three questions above. |

### Day 2: sit the test

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 min | Redo the items you missed yesterday, without the answers. |
| 2 | 10 min | Read the [timing guide](timing.md) and the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). |
| 3 | 10 min | Warm up with 10 mixed items you have not seen recently. Stop if you feel tired. |
| 4 | Test | Sit the real test in a quiet room, on a desktop or laptop, at a time when you are usually sharp. |

If you only have one evening, do day 1 blocks 1, 2 and 4, then sit the test the next morning.

## What not to do

- **Do not hunt for "real" questions.** The BMCT-II draws each candidate's items from a bank, so recalled questions will not match yours. Learn the rules instead.
- **Do not memorise formulas you do not understand.** The test is about predicting what happens. A rule you can picture is worth more than an equation.
- **Do not skip the small topics.** Every test includes a set amount of each content type, so heat, electricity, sound and volumes all appear.
- **Do not cram the night before.** A tired reader misses arrows and labels, which is where many marks go.
