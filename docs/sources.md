# Sources

Every fact in these notes about the test itself comes from the publisher, Pearson TalentLens, or from an independent test library or testing centre. Each source below was checked live on 24 November 2026. If something has changed, please [open an issue](https://github.com/mkhalid1/bennett-mechanical-study-notes/issues).

## What we could not confirm

- **Items per content area.** Pearson says each test includes a set amount of each content type, and its 2025 sheet mentions 12 domains, but the public pages we checked do not list all 12 or give counts. Prep sites publish splits. We could not verify them on a Pearson page, so we do not use them.
- **Number of answer options per item.** Not stated on Pearson's public pages. Our items use three options.
- **Calculator and scrap paper rules.** Not published by Pearson. Follow the employer's invitation.
- **Pass marks and retake rules.** Set by each employer, not by Pearson.

## What is our own guidance

These parts are our study advice, not publisher statements:

- the eight-topic grouping used to organise these notes
- the per-item timing budgets and clock checkpoints
- the "around 80% on timed practice" target
- the step-by-step methods, traps lists and study plans
- all practice items, diagrams and explanations, which are original
